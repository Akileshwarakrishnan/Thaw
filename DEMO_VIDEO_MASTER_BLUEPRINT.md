# THAW — Official Demo Video Master Production Blueprint & Teleprompter

> **Production Standard:** Broadcast & Silicon Valley FinTech Pitch Quality  
> **Target Duration:** 2 minutes 45 seconds to 3 minutes (Strictly under 3:00 for Devpost compliance)  
> **Target Speech Rate:** 130–140 words per minute (confident, clear, measured pace)  
> **Resolution:** 1080p (1920x1080) or 4K, 60fps  
> **Audio Target:** -14 LUFS, voice crisp with light compression, subtle ambient music at -24dB ducked  

---

## 🎬 Pre-Flight Recording Checklist (Do This Before Recording)

1. **Browser Setup:**
   - Open Chrome at [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000).
   - Set browser zoom to **110%** or **125%** (`Ctrl` + `+`) so every typography label is crystal clear on mobile judge screens.
   - Hide the bookmarks bar (`Ctrl` + `Shift` + `B`).
   - Use OBS Studio or Loom with **Window Capture** (so no desktop taskbars or notifications appear).
2. **Database Clean State:**
   - In terminal, run: `npm run seed` to ensure the Drifting Store (63/100 score) is loaded as the default state.
3. **Audio Setup:**
   - Use a directional USB/XLR mic placed 6 inches from mouth with pop filter.
   - Speak with energy and authority—you are pitching a software that protects hundreds of thousands of dollars in merchant cash flow.

## 🎨 Video Editing & Post-Production Guidelines (10-Year Editor Standard)

1. **Cursor Smoothing:**
   - In your video editor (ScreenFlow, Camtasia, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, or CapCut), turn on smooth cursor motion or add a subtle soft-circle highlight around clicks.
2. **Dynamic Zoom Transitions:**
   - When introducing the Risk Gauge: **Slow zoom (115%) over 1.5 seconds**.
   - When clicking *Approve & Execute*: **Punch-in to 125%** focused on the button, then pan smoothly up to the green notification banner and the dropping gauge.
3. **Sound Design (Foley):**
   - Add a subtle, high-end "click" or soft UI chime when *Approve & Execute* is clicked.
   - When the score drops, add a subtle deep bass drop or sweep down.
4. **Text Callouts (Lower Thirds):**
   - At 0:30: Lower-third title: `THAW | Deterministic 6-Signal Underwriting Engine`
   - At 1:40: Lower-third title: `PAYPAL AGENT TOOLKIT | Approval-Gated MCP Dispatch`
   - At 2:25: Lower-third title: `100% REAL SANDBOX PROOF | 5 Core PayPal REST APIs`
5. **Color & Export Settings:**
   - Color space: Rec. 709
   - Bitrate: 18–25 Mbps (VBR 2-pass)
   - Codec: H.264 / MP4
